Real-world assets (RWAs) are tokenized representations of off-chain instruments: equities, ETFs, commodities, and treasuries. 0x provides neutral on-chain infrastructure for trading them, so you can offer tokenized stocks and gold alongside crypto-native assets without standing up a second execution stack.

RWAs are available through the APIs you already use. If you have a Swap API integration, adding RWAs is a configuration change, not a rewrite.

<Note>
  RWA trading is **blocked by default**. Your team must opt in once before any
  RWA quote will be returned. See [Enable RWA
  Trading](/rwa/guides/enable-rwa-trading).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Evaluating 0x for RWAs? See the [RWA solution
  page](https://0x.org/solution/rwas) for supported assets, execution quality,
  and teams already live.
</Tip>

## Why RWAs are different

Tokenized real-world assets trade by different rules than crypto-native tokens, and that shapes how 0x handles them:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **They are regulated instruments.** Access is gated on your team explicitly
    opting in and accepting the RWA trading agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Liquidity is thinner and more concentrated.** RWA markets do not have the
    deep AMM pools that major crypto pairs do, so routing leans more heavily on
    professional market makers via [0x RFQ](/evm/0x-swap-api/additional-topics/about-the-rfq-system).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **You control who can trade them.** You are responsible for restricting
    access on your platform by jurisdiction. See [Your
    responsibilities](#your-responsibilities).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can trade

0x supports a broad range of RWAs rather than a fixed list of issuers. 0x identifies RWA tokens using third-party data vendors. When a new asset is indexed and has liquidity 0x can route, it is typically quotable through the API.

## Your responsibilities

The RWA trading agreement requires you to prevent access by users located in:

- The United States
- Any jurisdiction subject to comprehensive sanctions
- Any jurisdiction where offering the relevant asset is prohibited by applicable law
- Any jurisdiction excluded by the asset's issuer

How you enforce this is up to you, for example geo-blocking restricted regions and applying any issuer-specific rules. A common pattern is to keep the token visible but disable trading, with a message such as "NVDA is not available for trading in your region at this time."

## How 0x handles RWAs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="New RWA tokens are indexed automatically">
  0x identifies new RWA tokens through its data vendors, so newly issued assets
  with routable liquidity become quotable without a manual allowlist.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access is opt-in at the team level">
  RWAs are blocked by default. Your team opts in once, and that opt-in applies
  to every app and API key on the team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Thin markets route to professional market makers">
  For regulated and less liquid markets, 0x routes to professional market makers
  through RFQ rather than relying on public AMM liquidity alone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Same multi-chain coverage">
  RWA trading runs across 0x's supported chains. See [Supported Chains and
  Providers](/docs/introduction/supported-chains) for the current list.
